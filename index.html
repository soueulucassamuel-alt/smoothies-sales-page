The idea

Two minutes of quiet.
One glass of nutrients.

Most mornings ask a lot of your brain before you've given it anything back. This book pairs each smoothie with a short, calming practice — so the ritual, not just the recipe, is what sets the tone for your day.

Every recipe was chosen for what it puts to work: antioxidants that guard brain cells from oxidative stress, healthy fats that support cell structure, and the kind of steady energy that doesn't crash by 10am.

Why these ingredients

Every recipe is built around a short list of well-studied nutrients.

No exotic superfoods — just produce you already know, chosen for what the research says it does.

Bb

Blueberries

Rich in anthocyanins, antioxidants that help protect brain cells from oxidative stress.

Av

Avocado

Monounsaturated fats and vitamin E support healthy blood flow and protect cell membranes.

Tm

Turmeric

Curcumin, paired with black pepper for absorption, brings anti-inflammatory support to the mix.

Bt

Beet & Chia

Nitrates that support blood flow, plus omega-3s that support cell structure and communication.
